Our approach

Nothing is templated

The decoration industry in this region usually runs on fixed packages: the same arch, the same lights, the same backdrop, scaled up or down. We don't do that. Every event starts with a conversation about your venue, your guest count and what you actually want it to feel like.

Our palette leans into deep royal purple, metallic gold and warm ivory — colours that photograph well under both evening lights and daylight, and that suit the kind of celebrations we're usually asked to decorate.

How we work

  • You send us your date, venue and a reference photo — on WhatsApp or in the form
  • We come back with ideas and a quote, usually the same day
  • We visit or review the venue photos before finalising the design
  • Setup happens early on the day, coordinated by one point of contact throughout
  • We clear after the event unless you've asked us to stay

On pricing

We don't publish prices on this website, deliberately. Decoration cost moves a lot with florals, material, venue size and guest count — a number on a website would either be wrong or a trap. Talk to us and you'll get an honest figure tailored to what you have in mind.
