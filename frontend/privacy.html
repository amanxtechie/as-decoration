Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this site, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Enquiry forms: your name, phone number, and optionally email, event date, occasion and description.
  • Reviews and photo submissions: the name, location, text and images you choose to send.
  • Your area: the district and town you pick, stored only in your own browser.
  • Saved designs: stored only in your own browser, never sent to us.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry or to discuss an event you're planning. We do not sell them, and we do not add you to any marketing list. If you'd like your details removed, message us on WhatsApp and we'll delete them.

What stays on your device

Your chosen area and your saved designs are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your phone or computer, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Photos you submit

Photos you upload are reviewed by us before appearing publicly. You can ask us to remove any photo at any time.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses local storage only, for the features described above.
